Scams We Detect

ScamPay identifies 7+ types of digital financial fraud

🔑

OTP Scam

Fraudsters trick you into sharing your One-Time Password to steal funds.

📋

KYC Scam

Fake KYC update requests demanding personal documents or payments.

💳

UPI Fraud

Collect requests, fake QR codes, and suspicious UPI payment links.

📞

Fake Customer Care

Impersonators of banks, PayTM, PhonePe, or NPCI support numbers.

🏆

Lottery/Reward

"You've won ₹10 lakh!" — fake prize scams asking for processing fees.

📈

Investment Fraud

Guaranteed high-return schemes on Telegram or WhatsApp groups.

🎣

Phishing Links

Malicious URLs mimicking bank or e-commerce login pages.

💸

Refund Scam

Fake refund agents asking you to scan a QR to "receive" money.
