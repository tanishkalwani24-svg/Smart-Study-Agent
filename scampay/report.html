Official Reporting Portals

🏛️

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Government of India's official portal for reporting cyber crimes including financial fraud.

1 Visit the portal
2 Click "Report Cyber Crime"
3 Fill complaint form
4 Note complaint number
Visit Portal →
📡

Chakshu Portal (TRAI)

Report suspected fraud communications — SMS, calls, WhatsApp messages to TRAI's Sanchar Saathi.

1 Open Chakshu Portal
2 Select communication type
3 Enter suspect number/details
4 Submit report
Report on Chakshu →
📞

1930 — Cyber Fraud Helpline

India's national helpline for cyber financial fraud. Available 24/7 for immediate assistance.

1 Dial 1930
2 Describe the fraud
3 Provide transaction details
4 Follow officer instructions

🛡️ What To Do If You've Been Scammed

0–30 min

Immediate Action

  • Call 1930 immediately
  • Contact your bank to freeze transactions
  • Change UPI PIN / Net Banking password
  • Don't make any more payments
1–24 hrs

File Formal Complaint

  • File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in
  • Visit nearest police station
  • Get complaint acknowledgment number
  • Preserve all screenshots and messages
1–7 days

Follow Up

  • Follow up with bank chargeback
  • Track complaint status online
  • Report on Chakshu portal (TRAI)
  • Alert family and friends
